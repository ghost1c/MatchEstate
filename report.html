Before Reporting

Before submitting a report, please make sure that:

  • You are using the latest version of MatchEstate.
  • You have completed the initial setup.
  • Your Google Gemini API Key is valid.
  • You have reviewed the Help Center for common solutions.

If your issue is related to installation, permissions, or configuration, please visit our Support Center first.
