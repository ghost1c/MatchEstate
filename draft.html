How to Opt Out

If you are participating in the MatchEstate draft testing program and no longer wish to receive test versions, simply contact our support team and request to be removed from the tester list.

Support

Email: support@matchestate.ai

Please include the Google account you used for testing so we can remove your access as quickly as possible.

What Happens Next?

After your request has been processed, you will no longer receive access to draft versions of MatchEstate. This does not affect any future public releases available through the Google Workspace Marketplace.
