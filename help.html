Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install MatchEstate?

Install MatchEstate from Google Workspace Marketplace and open it from Google Sheets.

How do I start using AI Matching?

Complete the initial setup, authorize your Google Account, enter your personal Google Gemini API Key, and begin matching customer requirements with your property portfolio.

Where can I get a Google Gemini API Key?

You can generate your own API key by visiting Google AI Studio.

Open Google AI Studio

My matching process failed

Please verify the following before contacting support:

  • Your Google Gemini API Key is valid.
  • Your internet connection is working.
  • You have authorized MatchEstate using your Google Account.
  • Your Google Sheets file is accessible.

Google Workspace Permissions

MatchEstate requests only the minimum Google Workspace permissions required to provide its functionality. The application accesses only the Google Sheets and Google Drive files that you explicitly authorize. No data is accessed without your consent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does MatchEstate store my Google data?

No. MatchEstate processes only the information required to provide the requested functionality.


Can MatchEstate access all my Google Drive files?

No. MatchEstate accesses only the Google Sheets and Google Drive files that you explicitly authorize.


Which AI model does MatchEstate use?

MatchEstate uses Google's Gemini AI models to compare customer requirements with property portfolios and generate AI-assisted reports.


Who is MatchEstate designed for?

MatchEstate is designed for real estate agents, brokers, agencies, and property consultants.
