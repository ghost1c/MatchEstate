Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Google Gemini API Key?

Yes. MatchEstate requires your own Google Gemini API Key to perform AI-powered property matching.


Can MatchEstate access all my Google Drive files?

No. MatchEstate accesses only the Google Sheets and Google Drive files that you explicitly authorize.


Can I revoke MatchEstate's permissions?

Yes. You can revoke MatchEstate's access at any time through your Google Account security settings.


Where can I report a bug?

Please visit our Report an Issue page or submit an issue through our official GitHub repository.
