About MatchEstate

MatchEstate is an AI-powered Google Workspace Add-on designed specifically for real estate professionals. The application enables agents and agencies to store property portfolios in Google Sheets, compare customer requirements with available listings, generate AI-powered compatibility scores, and produce professional property matching reports using Google's Gemini AI. MatchEstate helps reduce manual work, improve customer satisfaction, and streamline the entire property matching process.

Main Features

AI Property Matching

Automatically compare customer requirements with your property portfolio using Google Gemini AI.

Google Sheets Integration

Manage your entire property portfolio directly inside Google Sheets.

Google Drive Reports

Generate professional AI-assisted reports and save them securely to your own Google Drive.

Professional Analysis

Receive ranked property recommendations together with AI-generated explanations.

How MatchEstate Works

MatchEstate simplifies the property matching process in four easy steps.

1. Create Your Portfolio

Store and maintain your property portfolio in Google Sheets.

2. Enter Customer Requirements

Specify customer preferences such as location, budget, property type, and additional requirements.

3. AI Analysis

Google Gemini AI analyzes customer requirements and compares them with your portfolio to calculate compatibility scores.

4. Review Results

Receive ranked property recommendations together with AI explanations and downloadable reports.

Why MatchEstate Requests Google Permissions

MatchEstate requests only the minimum Google Workspace permissions required to provide its core functionality.

  • Access Google Sheets selected by the user.
  • Create AI-powered property matching reports.
  • Save generated reports to the user's own Google Drive.
  • Authenticate users securely using Google OAuth.
  • Never access files without explicit user authorization.
  • Users can revoke permissions at any time from their Google Account.
  • MatchEstate never sells, shares, or uses Google Workspace data for advertising.

Privacy & Security

MatchEstate follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and requests only the minimum permissions required to provide the requested functionality. User data remains under the user's Google Account and is processed only to perform AI-powered property matching and generate reports. MatchEstate does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
