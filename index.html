About MatchEstate

MatchEstate is an AI-powered Google Workspace Add-on designed specifically for real estate professionals. The application allows agents to store their property portfolio in Google Sheets, compare customer requirements with available properties, generate AI compatibility scores, and create professional reports using Google's Gemini AI. MatchEstate helps agencies save time, improve customer satisfaction, and simplify the property matching process.

Main Features

AI Property Matching

Automatically compare customer preferences with your property listings using Gemini AI.

Google Sheets Integration

Manage your portfolio directly inside Google Sheets without exporting data.

Google Drive Reports

Generate AI-assisted reports and save them directly to your own Google Drive.

Professional Analysis

Receive ranked property recommendations together with AI explanations.

How MatchEstate Works

MatchEstate simplifies the real estate matching process in four easy steps.

1. Store Properties

Create and maintain your property portfolio inside Google Sheets.

2. Enter Customer Requirements

Specify the customer's preferences, budget, location, property type and other requirements.

3. AI Analysis

Google Gemini AI compares customer requirements with your portfolio and calculates compatibility scores.

4. Review Results

Receive ranked property recommendations together with AI explanations and professional reports.

Why MatchEstate Requests Google Permissions

MatchEstate requests only the minimum Google permissions necessary to provide its core functionality.

  • Read property portfolio data from Google Sheets selected by the user.
  • Create AI-generated matching reports.
  • Save reports to the user's own Google Drive.
  • Authenticate securely using Google OAuth.
  • Never access files that the user has not explicitly selected.
  • Never sell, share, or use Google user data for advertising.
  • All permissions are used only to provide the requested functionality.

Privacy & Security

MatchEstate follows Google's API Services User Data Policy and OAuth requirements. Only the minimum permissions required by the application are requested. User data remains under the user's Google account and is processed only to perform property matching and generate reports requested by the user. MatchEstate does not sell personal information and does not access unrelated Google content.

Frequently Asked Questions

No. MatchEstate only accesses files that you explicitly authorize.

Which AI model is used?

MatchEstate uses Google's Gemini AI models to perform property matching and generate reports.

Who is MatchEstate for?

MatchEstate is designed for real estate agents, brokers and property professionals.
