About MatchEstate

MatchEstate is an AI-powered Google Workspace Add-on developed specifically for real estate professionals. The application enables agents and agencies to manage property portfolios in Google Sheets, compare customer requirements with available listings, calculate AI-powered compatibility scores using Google's Gemini AI, and generate professional property matching reports. MatchEstate helps reduce manual work, improve customer satisfaction, and simplify the entire property matching workflow.

Main Features

AI Property Matching

Automatically compare customer requirements with your property portfolio using Google Gemini AI.

Google Sheets Integration

Manage your complete property portfolio directly inside Google Sheets without exporting data.

Google Drive Reports

Generate professional AI-assisted reports and save them directly to your own Google Drive.

Professional Analysis

Receive ranked property recommendations together with detailed AI-generated explanations.

How MatchEstate Works

MatchEstate simplifies the property matching process in four easy steps.

1. Build Your Portfolio

Create and maintain your property portfolio directly in Google Sheets.

2. Enter Customer Requirements

Specify your customer's preferred location, budget, property type, size, and other requirements.

3. AI Analysis

Google Gemini AI analyzes customer requirements and compares them with your property portfolio to calculate compatibility scores.

4. Review Results

Receive ranked property recommendations together with AI-generated explanations and downloadable reports.

Why MatchEstate Requests Google Permissions

MatchEstate requests only the minimum Google Workspace permissions required to provide its core functionality.

  • Access only the Google Sheets files that you explicitly authorize.
  • Create AI-powered property matching reports.
  • Save generated reports to your own Google Drive when requested.
  • Authenticate users securely using Google OAuth.
  • Never access files without your explicit permission.
  • Users can revoke access at any time through their Google Account settings.
  • MatchEstate never sells Google user data or uses it for advertising.

Privacy & Security

MatchEstate follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Only the minimum Google Workspace permissions required for the requested functionality are used. Google Workspace data is processed only to compare customer requirements with property portfolios and generate reports requested by the user. MatchEstate does not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does MatchEstate store my Google Workspace data?

No. MatchEstate processes only the information required to provide the functionality you request.

Can MatchEstate access all of my Google Drive files?

No. MatchEstate accesses only the Google Sheets and Google Drive files that you explicitly authorize.

Which AI model does MatchEstate use?

MatchEstate uses Google's Gemini AI models to analyze customer requirements, compare property portfolios, and generate AI-assisted reports.

Who is MatchEstate designed for?

MatchEstate is designed for real estate agents, brokers, agencies, and property professionals.
